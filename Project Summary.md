# Book of the Silver Vigil — Project Summary

## Purpose of This File

This is the working project summary for **Book of the Silver Vigil**. It is the review point for the story before new material is pushed into the main book manuscript. It records established canon, character facts, story direction, unresolved questions, and writing rules so that future chapters remain consistent.

This file is a **living project document**. New decisions should be added here when they become canon. Material that is only brainstorming should remain clearly marked as such until approved.

---

## Book

**Working title:** *Book of the Silver Vigil*

**Setting:** Forgotten Realms / D&D-inspired fantasy

**Format:** Novel, not a D&D campaign.

**Narrative approach:** Classic epic fantasy with atmosphere, character-driven action, dialogue, gradual revelations, and mysteries that unfold naturally through the story.

---

## Protagonist — Bastian

- **Name used in the narrative:** Bastian
- **Family name:** Evander
- **Important naming rule:** The surname **Evander must not appear in the narrative until the final part of the book**. His family are the only people who know it, and it is kept secret because of his profession.
- **Race:** Human
- **Public profession:** Adventurer / wandering cleric
- **Secret profession:** Moonwarden
- **Home:** Baldur's Gate
- **Affiliation:** Temple of the Silver Vigil
- **Order:** Order of the Moonwardens

### Personality and Motivation

Bastian is practical, disciplined, capable, and comfortable with the life he was raised to lead. He does **not** resent his upbringing or his profession. He likes being a Moonwarden and is comfortable with the fact that the work is dangerous and covert.

He appreciates money, luxury, comfort, excellent equipment, good weapons, fine armor, good food, comfortable beds, and quality inns. These preferences are simply part of his personality rather than a moral lesson or deep character flaw.

His immediate goal after exile is straightforward: **break the witches' curse, return to the Temple of the Silver Vigil, regain his standing, and resume his life as a Moonwarden.** He wants his old life back.

Bastian normally hunts werewolves alone. He is accustomed to solitary work and should not behave like someone who routinely travels with an adventuring party.

### Public Cover

Bastian travels openly as a cleric and adventurer. His Moonwarden work remains secret because revealing that he is hunting a particular target would compromise the hunt. His public identity allows him to travel, investigate, and gather information without announcing what he is actually looking for.

He would never casually announce that he is hunting witches, werewolves, or a particular quarry.

---

## The Temple and the Moonwardens

The **Temple of the Silver Vigil** is a prominent Selûnite temple in Baldur's Gate.

The **Order of the Moonwardens** is a hereditary order within the temple dedicated to hunting wolves, werewolves, and other lycanthropes. Bastian was born into the family caste that produces Moonwardens and was trained from birth for the profession.

His upbringing was harsh, disciplined, and utilitarian, but Bastian does not view it as abuse or something he escaped. It was the life he was given, the life he chose to embrace, and the life he intends to return to.

The Moonwardens operate covertly. Their secrecy is operational rather than shame-based: a target who knows a Moonwarden is hunting them can flee, alter its behavior, hide evidence, or otherwise make the hunt more difficult.

---

## The Curse and Exile

Bastian was infected during a conflict with **witches**.

The resulting affliction is **not ordinary lycanthropy**. It is a specific witches' curse that the Temple of the Silver Vigil cannot remove or control through its usual methods.

Bastian returned to the temple for cleansing. The rites failed. The priests and healers could not identify a conventional method of reversing what had been done to him.

The curse eventually caused a **terrible aftermath in Baldur's Gate**, including death and destruction. The aftermath, rather than moral condemnation, was the reason Bastian was exiled.

The order did not exile him because they believed him evil or traitorous. They could not reliably control the curse and could not risk another incident. Bastian understands the decision because, if their positions were reversed, he would have been trained to make the same choice.

He leaves the only home he has known.

### The Moonwarden Hunt

Bastian knows that another Moonwarden will eventually be sent after him. He does **not** know who that hunter will be.

He knows only that the hunter will know who he is and will be one of his own.

This creates a continuing pressure throughout the book: Bastian is pursuing the witches while knowing that the Order of the Moonwardens is pursuing him.

---

## The Two Hunts

The story is driven by two overlapping hunts:

1. **Bastian's hunt:** Find the witches and discover how to remove the curse.
2. **The Moonwarden hunt:** The Order eventually sends one of its own to find Bastian.

Neither hunt should resolve quickly. Both should develop gradually through encounters, clues, wrong turns, discoveries, and changing relationships.

The witches' actual coven/lair should be **far away and encountered near the end of the book**. Early leads must not take Bastian directly to the coven.

---

## Nalara

**Nalara** is a **moon elf druid of the Circle of the Moon**.

- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Race:** Moon elf
- **Class:** Druid
- **Circle:** Circle of the Moon
- **Role:** Companion and significant character
- **Relationship to Bastian:** Genuine developing relationship; attraction and rapport should develop naturally.

Nalara is attractive, alluring, intelligent, observant, independent, and self-possessed.

She is **not** a wolf, werewolf, Moonwarden, or agent of the Order. She is not responsible for Bastian's exile and is not seeking him in order to turn him in or hunt him.

Nalara has her **own reason for seeking Bastian**. That reason is somehow connected to why she is looking for him and may involve Bastian unknowingly leading her somewhere important.

The deeper connection should remain hidden until the audience is firmly hooked. The eventual revelation may come very late in the book, potentially near the end.

Nalara may tell Bastian things that are technically true while withholding the whole truth. Bastian's mistrust of her should come from her behavior rather than from an obvious betrayal: she may be too perceptive, know oddly specific things, appear where she should not, ask unusual questions, or understand things about the moon, transformation, nature, or curses that seem beyond what she should know.

Her motives should not make her obviously villainous.

### Thematic Contrast

Nalara's Circle of the Moon background gives her an understanding of transformation as something natural and sacred. Bastian's transformation is unnatural and imposed upon him by a witch's curse. This contrast can deepen their relationship without turning it into an exposition device.

Nalara's druidic Wild Shape is distinct from Bastian's cursed condition. She is not secretly another kind of werewolf.

## Current Story Position

Chapter One establishes Bastian's exile from the Temple of the Silver Vigil and his departure from Baldur's Gate.

The current continuation has Bastian traveling north while quietly gathering information about witches and unusual incidents connected to his curse. He hears stories of disappearances and a caravan attack, follows the trail to an old clearing, and finds a small piece of finely worked metal that he cannot identify. He keeps it because it is well made, not because he understands its significance.

He eventually reaches a small settlement and speaks with an herb trader named **Harven**, who tells him that another traveler recently asked similar questions and headed north toward the marsh.

The current chapter ends with Bastian following that trail north.

The marsh is a **lead, not the witches' coven**.

---

## Current Clue

A small piece of worked metal was found near an old fire pit connected to the caravan trail.

Bastian does not recognize it and does not know what it means. It is not presented to the reader as an obvious magical artifact or plot device. Its significance should remain subtle.

Nobody else currently knows what it means.

Bastian keeps it partly because he has an appreciation for well-made things.

## Story Structure

The planned story should be substantial enough to support a full novel rather than resolving the central conflict in only a few chapters.

The journey should include:

- intermediate leads;
- strangers and recurring characters;
- towns, roads, wilderness, and other locations;
- investigations and conversations;
- wrong turns;
- discoveries that create new questions;
- moments where Bastian's plans do not work;
- gradual character development;
- the eventual arrival of Nalara;
- the developing relationship between Bastian and Nalara;
- increasing pressure from the Moonwarden pursuing Bastian;
- revelations about the curse;
- revelations about Nalara's reason for seeking him;
- eventual confrontation with the witches' coven far from Baldur's Gate;
- resolution of the curse and Bastian's attempt to reclaim his former life.

The story should not rush from clue to clue. Travel, character interaction, atmosphere, and complications should have room to breathe.

1. **Page of Cups — reversed**
2. **Path A: Three of Cups — reversed**
3. **Path B: Knight of Cups — upright**
4. **Hidden Influence: Two of Cups — reversed**
5. **Guidance: Seven of Swords — reversed**

The useful narrative themes are:

- isolation and broken fellowship;
- the possibility of a new connection;
- a relationship that is not initially understood correctly;
- hidden or misaligned motives;
- secrets that eventually come to light;
- concealment becoming increasingly difficult;
- eventual confession, exposure, or unraveling of deception.

The tarot should **guide the story without appearing in the novel**. The narrator should never explain the story structure through tarot symbolism.

## Writing Rules

### Paragraphing

Use traditional novel paragraphing.

Narrative paragraphs should generally contain multiple sentences that develop a thought, action, description, or exchange. **Do not use isolated one-sentence narrative paragraphs for dramatic effect.** Do not use fragment paragraphs or trailer-style line breaks.

Dialogue may be separated naturally when speakers change, but surrounding narration should remain flowing prose.

### Style

Aim for classic epic-fantasy readability and atmosphere while keeping the prose original.

Prioritize:

- flowing prose;
- atmosphere;
- character-driven action;
- natural dialogue;
- scene-based exposition;
- gradual revelations;
- subtle clues;
- believable cause and effect;
- mysteries that invite the reader forward.

Avoid:

- list-like prose;
- excessive exposition;
- repeated descriptions of Bastian's skills or training;
- obvious foreshadowing;
- "chosen one" language;
- convenient dossiers, records, shrines, or other artificial information dumps;
- immediate access to the witches' coven;
- making Nalara obviously evil;
- making Bastian aimless or self-pitying;
- repeatedly explaining facts the reader already knows.

Bastian's abilities should be shown naturally when they matter rather than repeatedly explained.

The curse and the witches do not need to be reintroduced every time they appear in the story. The reader already understands the central problem. Focus on what Bastian is discovering now.

## Canon Naming Rules

- **Bastian** is the name used throughout almost the entire narrative.
- **Evander** is reserved for the final part of the book.
- **Temple of the Silver Vigil** is the proper name of the temple.
- **Order of the Moonwardens** is the proper name of the order.
- **Moonwardens** is the common short form.
- **Nalara** is the companion's established name.
- Nalara is a **moon elf**, not a wolf or werewolf.
- Nalara is a druid of the **Circle of the Moon**.
- Common nouns such as *human*, *cleric*, *moon elf*, *druid*, and *adventurer* remain lowercase.

## Things Not Yet Canon

The following should **not** be treated as established unless explicitly approved later:

- the exact identity of the Moonwarden sent after Bastian;
- the exact nature and full rules of the witches' curse;
- the identity and history of the witches' coven;
- the exact reason Nalara is seeking Bastian;
- the deeper destination or connection Nalara may be leading toward;
- the meaning of the worked-metal clue;
- the final resolution of Bastian's curse;
- the exact final fate of Bastian's standing within the Temple of the Silver Vigil;
- any invented place name such as **Briar Glen**.

These are areas for development, not settled facts.
